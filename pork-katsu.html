Pork Katsu

A delicious katsu dish that skips the flour, improving the panko stick and reducing calories.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless pork chops
  • 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Recipe

  1. Season the pork chops with salt and pepper.
  2. Dredge the pork chops in the beaten eggs, then coat with panko breadcrumbs.
  3. Heat vegetable oil in a skillet and fry the pork chops until golden brown.
  4. Serve hot with your favorite dipping sauce.
